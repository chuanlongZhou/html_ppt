## 2 · why-resolution — Why high-resolution emission inventory

Slide flow / 画面流程: `Total Emission` → (click) photo fades in → (click) five sector labels with round-head leader lines → (click) arrow + `Policy` → (click) `?` beside the arrow → (click) photo turns grey, `Policy` sinks to the bottom, an empty `Information Gap` frame opens between → (3 clicks) three explanation cards fill the frame → (click) zoom on the highway; circle and *Which roads? / Which time? / Which vehicles?* play automatically → (click) zoom on housing; three notes in one column point at the tallest tower / another apartment block / a small house → (click) everything on the left disappears and the finale plays automatically: the five sector labels with leader lines on the coloured photo first, then the three boxes with arrows (Near-real-time activities / Accurate emission factors → High-resolution Sectoral Emission Inventory → Enables Spatially Explicit Research & Policy).

**State 0–1 · Total Emission → Policy**

- EN: When we see a city's total carbon emissions, or the contributions of transport, power, industry, residential buildings and aviation, it is easy to think that these numbers already reflect concrete human activity, and that we can derive what policy to adopt directly from them.
- 中: 当我们看到一个城市的碳排放总量，或者交通、电力、工业、住宅和航空各自的排放贡献时，很容易认为：这些数字已经反映了城市中具体的人类活动，因此我们可以直接从这些数据推导出应该采取什么政策。

**State 2 · Information Gap**

- EN: But between emission accounting and urban action, there is still an information gap.
- 中: 但从排放核算到城市行动，中间仍然存在一个信息缺口。

**State 3 · Three cards (top-down statistics → proxies and profiles → regional map)**

- EN: Most existing inventories rely on top-down statistics, such as annual energy consumption or industrial output. Spatial proxies and typical time profiles then distribute these emissions across places and hours. These methods give an important basis for total accounting and trend analysis, but they cannot tell us when, where and through which behaviour these emissions actually occurred.
- 中: 大部分现存的排放清单依赖自上而下的“统计数据”，比如年度能源消费、工业产量等汇总活动数据，再通过空间代理和典型时间曲线，将排放分配到不同地点和时段。这些方法为总量核算和趋势分析提供了重要基础，但我们并不能通过这些清单确定这些排放到底是在什么时候、在什么地方、由什么行为导致的。

**State 4 · Roads (Which roads? Which time? Which vehicles?)**

- EN: For example, knowing the annual emissions of the transport sector is not enough to decide which roads, which time slots, which vehicles and which traffic conditions deserve priority.
- 中: 例如，知道交通部门一年的排放量，还不足以判断哪些道路、哪些时段、哪些车辆和交通条件最值得优先干预。

**State 5 · Residential (income / infrastructure / climate risk)** — *replaces the industrial example; the sentence below is drafted by Claude, please confirm / 替换了原来的工业例子，下面这句由 Claude 起草，请确认*

- EN: The same holds for housing: a residential total hides the differences between income levels, infrastructure and climate risks across neighbourhoods.
- 中: 住宅部门也是如此：一个住宅排放总量，掩盖了不同街区在收入水平、基础设施和气候风险上的差异。

**State 6 · New paradigm (plays automatically: sector labels first, then three boxes + arrows)**

- EN: This is why we need high-resolution emission inventories, built from near-real-time activity data and accurate emission factors. They link emissions to specific places, times and activities, giving governments and researchers a foundation to identify hotspots, design targeted measures, and evaluate the changes after implementation.
- 中: 因此，我们需要由近实时活动数据和准确排放因子构建的高分辨率排放清单，把排放与具体地点、时间和活动联系起来，为政府和研究者识别热点、设计有针对性的措施，以及评估实施后的变化提供依据。
